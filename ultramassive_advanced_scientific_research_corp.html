Strategy

Deterministic AI, or it doesn’t ship.

Most failures in production AI are not model failures; they are operational failures: no telemetry, no threshold, no audit trail, no owner. Our strategy is to treat inference as a routed, metered, observable utility.

01

Route, don’t guess

Every request is scored against a possibility–necessity band before a model is chosen. Tiers are admitted on possibility; side effects gate on necessity. No point estimate decides alone.

02

Prove it in the open

The console ships as open source, and the mathematics behind every panel resolves to a public preprint. If a claim cannot be traced to a citation and a formula, it does not reach the screen.

03

Operate it like infrastructure

Models are not a feature; they are a fleet. Drift, saturation, provenance, and failover belong in a network operations centre, not a notebook.

04

Price the externality

Placement is a carbon decision as much as a latency decision. Grid intensity is a first-class routing input, not an annual report footnote.

Cost

Reduce blended inference spend by routing the majority of traffic to the smallest sufficient tier.

Latency

Hold routing overhead well below the inference cost it displaces.

Auditability

Every routing decision replayable from a stable, versioned decision contract.

Carbon

Steer elastic workloads toward low-intensity grids without breaching SLO.
